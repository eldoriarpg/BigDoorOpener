# Big Doors Opener

[![Release](https://img.shields.io/nexus/maven-releases/de.eldoria/big-doors-opener?label=Release&logo=Release&server=https%3A%2F%2Feldonexus.de&style=for-the-badge)][release]
[![Snapshot](https://img.shields.io/nexus/s/de.eldoria/big-doors-opener?color=orange&label=Snapshot&server=https%3A%2F%2Feldonexus.de&style=for-the-badge)][snapshot]

An add-on for [Big Doors](https://modrinth.com/plugin/bigdoors) that opens and closes doors on its own: when players
come close, hold a key, have a permission, at certain times of day, on certain weather, after a cooldown and more.
Conditions can be combined freely.

## Requirements

- Paper 1.21.11 or newer
- Java 25
- Big Doors 0.1.8
- Optional: WorldGuard 7, PlaceholderAPI, MythicMobs, and NashornJs for custom evaluators and placeholder conditions

## Download

- [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/plugin/2mTWavvv)
- [Lyna](https://lyna.eldoria.de/products/3)

## Documentation

Setup, conditions, commands and permissions are documented on
[docs.eldoria.de/big-doors-opener](https://docs.eldoria.de/big-doors-opener). Updating from 2.x moves your doors into
`doors.yml` on the first start, see [Migration](https://docs.eldoria.de/big-doors-opener/migration).
